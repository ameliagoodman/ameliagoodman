Amelia Goodman

Good at: feminism, building things, organizing, reading.

I’m a software engineer, product builder, and founder of Bookworm Reads, a social book-tracking app built around the idea that reading is better together 🥰. It's like if Goodreads were prettier, gayer, and not owned by an evil empire. Bookworm is where a lot of the things I care about come together: community, playful and delightful software, and making the internet feel a little more human.

Over the years, I’ve been a fullstack engineer at Figma and Apple, worked with early-stage startups as a freelancer and technical advisor, and worked at the Stanford Computational Policy Lab building technology aimed at problems in the criminal justice system and other inequitable institutions.

I care deeply about social justice and equity, and I’m interested in helping communities build the technology we actually want, not just accepting the systems we’re given, all while centering delight and joy as much as possible. In my free time, I love making art – right now I'm particularly into knitting, sketching, and pottery – and riding my bike around San Francisco.
